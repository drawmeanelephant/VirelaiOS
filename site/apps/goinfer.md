Guest LLM inference on the GPU compute path (ADR 0053): every
matmul and the vocab softmax ride `sys_gpucomp` while norms, RoPE,
residuals and sampling stay on the CPU — the same split the ADR
pins. The bare launch is the windowed face over `virelai/infer`:
the deterministic stub journey needs no fetched weights, the model
journey loads an owner-approved SmolLM2 VIW, and the appver-1
envelope round-trips the armed journey and its log tail without
re-running it. `go-infer.spec` pins the stub transcript against a
golden byte-exact on real Metal. The tile is
[ICONINFR](https://github.com/drawmeanelephant/VirelaiOS/blob/main/image/art/icons/iconinfr.qoi).
