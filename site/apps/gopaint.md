The paint app — brush, line, rectangle, flood fill and eraser
over a fixed 16-colour palette at two brush sizes, on a draw-v2
document canvas with an undo ring, QOI save/open through the
shared FilePicker, and `x` for PNG export; a drop replaces the
canvas behind a confirm. Its declared title is the binary name on
purpose — the seat resolves drop-admission and session re-exec
through the recorded bin — and its row carries no `icon=` raster,
so the tile is the manifest's `P` rune. `go-paint.spec` exercises
all five tools by real pointer gesture.
