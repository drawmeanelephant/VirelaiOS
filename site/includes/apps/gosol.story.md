Klondike solitaire — draw-1 or draw-3 on a seeded deal, an
in-surface pointer drag that validates the drop and snaps back on
an illegal move, a double-click to the foundation, a 32-step undo
ring, a notify on the win, and a save_state blob that keeps the
whole table. `n` reseals a fresh deal, `d` toggles the draw mode.
Its legs are tagged in `go-games.spec` alongside Mines; the tile
is [ICONSOL](https://github.com/drawmeanelephant/VirelaiOS/blob/main/image/art/icons/iconsol.qoi).
