The file manager — a real Bubble Tea model over the window-bound
tty, not a widget mock-up: list and open on the share, batch
copy/move, trash with an undoable delete, recent files, Open With
dispatch through the shared mime registry, and spacebar QuickLook
previews (a `.vcf` paints as a parsed contact card). The `/` chord
runs a bounded content search over the folder subtree with `n`/`N`
walking the hits. `go-fileman.spec` drives a real rename over the
HID path and checks the re-list; the tile is
[ICONFILE](https://github.com/drawmeanelephant/VirelaiOS/blob/main/image/art/icons/iconfile.qoi).
