The emoji picker — a fuzzy-searchable grid of every glyph the
kernel's emoji font covers, plus the compose pairs and the kaomoji
dozen; Enter picks. A pick writes `text/plain` to the typed
clipboard lane and closes — or stays, per the toggle — and the
shelf's pinned favourites and recents ride the save_state v2
envelope. `go-emoji.spec` narrows 'fire' to the one glyph and
reads the clipboard policy boundary as the receipt. The tile is
[ICONEMOJ](https://github.com/drawmeanelephant/VirelaiOS/blob/main/image/art/icons/iconemoj.qoi).
