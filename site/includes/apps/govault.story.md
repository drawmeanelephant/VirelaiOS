The secrets store — one unlock-gated vault file
(PBKDF2-HMAC-SHA256 into AES-256-GCM, crash-safe writes) behind a
masked passphrase field: per-entry reveal, a copy that expires on
the seat's primary selection, an idle lock, a TOTP column, GVLX
export/import bundles that merge without clobbering, and its own
plaintext receipts face. No marker ever carries a name, a secret
or a passphrase. `go-vault.spec` runs the full
create-add-reveal-copy-expire-lock-unlock arc plus tamper legs;
the tile is [ICONVLT](https://github.com/drawmeanelephant/VirelaiOS/blob/main/image/art/icons/iconvlt.qoi).
